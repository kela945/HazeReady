# Outdoor event organisers: risk assessment against the 24-hour PSI forecast

Organisers of outdoor events are to conduct their own risk assessment, using the
24-hour PSI forecast together with the prevailing health advisories, to decide
whether an event proceeds.

Three features of this regime:

1. **The decision sits with the organiser.** No agency issues a
   proceed/cancel instruction at a given PSI value for general events.
2. **The 24-hour PSI forecast is the stated input**, not the current 1-hour
   PM2.5 reading — consistent with events running over many hours and being
   planned ahead.
3. **The forecast is a range.** Where the forecast range straddles a band
   boundary, the assessment must contend with both bands.

There is no published PSI threshold at which an outdoor event must be cancelled.
A system asked "at what PSI must I cancel my event?" cannot supply a number,
because no number is published; what it can supply is the assessment framework
and the health advisory that constrains participant exertion.
