# Air purifier specification and operation: coverage gap

This is a coverage note, not an agency publication.

The published guidance requires purifiers to be present — in every school
classroom, in the preschool room maintained for unwell children, and deployed in
healthcare facilities. It specifies nothing about how they are chosen or run.

Not published, and therefore not answerable from this corpus:

- Clean air delivery rate or capacity relative to room volume.
- Filter class (HEPA grade), replacement interval, or how to tell a filter is
  spent.
- Target indoor PM2.5 concentration, or how to verify the purifier is working.
- At what PSI the purifiers should be switched on, or whether they run
  continuously.
- Whether to run air-conditioning on recirculation, and window management.

These are indoor air quality engineering questions. Answering them from the haze
advisories would mean inventing a specification and presenting it as guidance.
The system abstains and routes to a building services or IAQ professional.
