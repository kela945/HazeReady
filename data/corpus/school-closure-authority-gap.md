# School closure decisions during haze

This is a coverage note, not an agency publication, and must not be cited as an
obligation source.

The published school and preschool haze measures address *activities* —
minimising outdoor activity, avoiding strenuous indoor exercise, purifier-
equipped rooms, monitoring unwell students. They do not publish a PSI value at
which schools close.

School closure is a decision made by MOE centrally and communicated directly to
schools during an episode. It is not derivable from a threshold table, and no
threshold table for it exists in this corpus.

A question of the form "at what PSI do schools close?" therefore has no answer
in the published guidance available to this system. The system must abstain and
point the asker to MOE's direct communications, rather than inferring a number
from the band structure.
