# Singapore Open Data Licence 1.0 terms

Datasets published on data.gov.sg are made available under the Singapore Open
Data Licence, which permits use, adaptation and redistribution of the data,
including commercially, subject to attribution of the source and of the agency,
and subject to the disclaimers of accuracy and liability in the licence.

The licence covers the *data* obtained through the API. It does not extend to
the text of advisories published on agency websites, which are separate works
under separate terms and are addressed individually in this corpus.

**Coverage note.** The licence summary here is authored from domain knowledge;
the licence text page itself was not retrieved by this system. Any redistribution
decision should be taken against the licence text rather than this summary.
