# Working out whether the vulnerable-person advisory applies to you

The advisory names four categories: elderly persons, pregnant women, children,
and persons with chronic lung disease or heart disease.

The categories are not numerically defined. No age is given for "elderly" or
"children", no gestational stage for pregnancy, and the qualifying chronic
conditions are not enumerated. This is a real limit of the published guidance,
not an omission in this corpus.

Where the categories clearly apply, apply them. Where a case is borderline —
whether well-controlled intermittent asthma counts as chronic lung disease, for
instance — the published guidance does not resolve it, and the question belongs
with a healthcare provider who knows the individual's history.

The difference the classification makes is one step of caution at 101–200
("reduce" becomes "minimise") and the N95 advisory above PSI 200. Treating
yourself as vulnerable when the case is borderline is the conservative choice
and carries little cost.

This is an authored interpretation.
