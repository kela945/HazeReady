# Static events and strenuous events are different exposures

The health advisory restricts prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion.
Two outdoor events at the same reading are therefore not equivalent.

| Event type | Exertion profile | Advisory bite at 101 – 200 |
|---|---|---|
| Competitive road race, mass sports | Prolonged and strenuous for all participants | Direct: healthy participants reduce, vulnerable minimise |
| Outdoor festival, seated audience | Prolonged, not strenuous | Partial: applies to vulnerable attendees and to working crew doing physical setup |
| Short ceremony, standing | Neither prolonged nor strenuous | Little |

At 201–300 all participants are to avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor
exertion, which a competitive race is by definition. At above 300 the advisory
extends to outdoor activity generally and reaches static events too.

Crew and volunteers are workers, and the MOM worksite guidance applies to them
independently of what applies to attendees. Setting up a stage at PSI 250 is
outdoor work.
