# Standing daily outdoor and gross motor activity requirement

ECDA requires preschools to incorporate outdoor activity into the curriculum:

- **Full-day preschools:** at least 30 minutes of outdoor activity daily, or 45
  minutes three times weekly.
- **Half-day preschools:** at least 30 minutes of gross motor activity three
  times weekly, with at least one session outdoors.

This is the baseline the haze guidance interrupts, and it is why the haze
instruction is to *replace* outdoor activity with alternatives rather than
simply delete it.

It also creates a tension the guidance does not resolve. If an episode runs for
two weeks, the standing outdoor requirement cannot be met, and no published
make-up or waiver policy addresses that. An operator asking how to reconcile the
two is asking a question the guidance does not answer.
