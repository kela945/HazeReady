# PSI bands and descriptors

The Pollutant Standards Index (PSI) is reported by NEA on a 24-hour averaging
basis. The index is divided into five bands.

| 24-hour PSI | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 0 – 50 | Good |
| 51 – 100 | Moderate |
| 101 – 200 | Unhealthy |
| 201 – 300 | Very Unhealthy |
| above 300 | Hazardous |

Band boundaries are inclusive of the stated endpoints. A 24-hour PSI reading of
exactly 100 is Moderate; a reading of 101 is Unhealthy. A reading of exactly 200
is Unhealthy; 201 is Very Unhealthy. A reading of exactly 300 is Very Unhealthy;
301 and above is Hazardous.

The band, not the raw number, is what governs published operational guidance.
Agencies publish obligations against the descriptor, so resolving the band
correctly is a precondition for applying any advisory in this corpus.
