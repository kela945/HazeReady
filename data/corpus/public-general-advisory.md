# General public: what to do at each band

| 24-hour PSI | Healthy persons | Vulnerable persons |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 50 (Good) | Normal activities | Normal activities |
| 51 – 100 (Moderate) | Normal activities | Normal activities |
| 101 – 200 (Unhealthy) | Reduce prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion | Minimise prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion |
| 201 – 300 (Very Unhealthy) | Avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion | Avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion; stay indoors where possible |
| above 300 (Hazardous) | Minimise outdoor activity | Avoid outdoor activity; remain indoors |

For an immediate personal decision — going for a run now — the 1-hour PM2.5
reading is the appropriate index rather than the 24-hour PSI.

Nothing in this advisory prohibits ordinary movement outdoors at any band below
Hazardous. The restrictions attach to *prolonged or strenuous exertion*.
