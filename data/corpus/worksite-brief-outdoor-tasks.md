# Brief and light outdoor tasks at high bands

The MOM band table attaches its restrictions to *prolonged or strenuous* outdoor
work at 101–200 and 201–300. Only above 300 does the restriction extend to
outdoor work generally.

A short, light outdoor task at 201–300 — a ten-minute equipment check — is
therefore not directly addressed by the band wording. This is not a permission.
It means the band table is silent and the employer's risk-assessment duty
governs: the assessment weighs the band, the individual's susceptibility, and
the intensity and duration of the task.

The correct answer to "can I send someone out for ten minutes at PSI 250?" is
not "yes, the restriction only covers prolonged work". It is that the published
band restriction does not resolve it, the risk assessment does, and a
susceptible employee should not be the one sent.

This is an authored interpretation of the scope of the published wording.
