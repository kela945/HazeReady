# Choosing between the forecast and the current reading

| The decision is about | Use | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Right now, short duration | 1-hour PM2.5, current | NEA designates it for immediate decisions |
| Right now, several hours | 24-hour PSI, current | Long-duration exposure tracks the averaged index |
| Later today or tomorrow | 24-hour PSI forecast (as a range) | NEA designates it for forward planning |

A decision made ahead of time against a forecast is provisional, and should be
re-checked against the actual reading before the activity begins.

The error to avoid is mixing them: taking a current 1-hour PM2.5 value and
treating it as the governing input for tomorrow's excursion, or taking a 24-hour
PSI forecast as authority for whether to step outside in the next ten minutes.
Both readings are correct; applying either to the wrong decision is not.
