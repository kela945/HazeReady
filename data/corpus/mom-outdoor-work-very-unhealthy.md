# Outdoor work measures at 24-hour PSI 201-300 (Very Unhealthy)

| Employee group | Required posture |
|---|---|
| Healthy employees | Avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor work |
| Susceptible employees | Avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor work |

At this band the guidance converges: both groups are to *avoid* prolonged or
strenuous outdoor work. The distinction between healthy and susceptible
employees that applies at 101–200 no longer produces different wording.

Where outdoor work is unavoidable, masks are to be provided.

Note the scope of the word "avoid". It attaches to *prolonged or strenuous*
outdoor work. Brief, light outdoor tasks are not addressed by this wording. An
employer asking whether a ten-minute outdoor inspection may proceed at PSI 250
is asking a question the published band guidance does not directly answer, and
the risk-assessment duty governs instead.
