# Eldercare cohorts are vulnerable by definition

Elderly persons are a named vulnerable group in the haze health advisory. An
eldercare facility's clients therefore fall under the vulnerable-person advisory
in its entirety, without the facility needing to assess individuals.

Applying the vulnerable-person rows:

| 24-hour PSI | Governing posture for the cohort |
|---|---|
| 0 – 100 | Normal activities |
| 101 – 200 | Minimise prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion |
| 201 – 300 | Avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion; stay indoors where possible |
| above 300 | Avoid outdoor activity; remain indoors |

Many eldercare clients additionally have chronic lung or heart disease, which
places them in the vulnerable category on a second independent ground. This does
not produce a further, stricter tier — the published advisory has only the two
populations.
