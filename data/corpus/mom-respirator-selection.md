# Respiratory protection selection and escalation for outdoor work

| 24-hour PSI | Respiratory protection expected |
|---|---|
| up to 200 | Not specified as a required control at this band |
| 201 – 300 | Provide masks where outdoor work is unavoidable |
| above 300 | Provide masks or appropriate respirators |
| above 400 | Full face respirators for prolonged outdoor work |

Masks are a control of last resort in the hierarchy, applied after the work has
already been reduced, rotated, or moved indoors as far as practicable. Issuing
masks is not a substitute for reducing exposure.

Two operational consequences:

- A mask programme requires fit testing and maintained stock, which is a
  preparatory activity, not something that can be arranged mid-episode.
- An employee who cannot tolerate the mask must be redeployed indoors. Working
  outdoors without the mask because the mask is uncomfortable is not an
  available option at these bands.
