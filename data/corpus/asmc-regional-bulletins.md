# ASEAN Specialised Meteorological Centre regional haze bulletins

The ASEAN Specialised Meteorological Centre, operated by Singapore's
Meteorological Service, publishes regional haze situation bulletins covering
hotspot counts, smoke plume observations and the prevailing wind regime across
the ASEAN region.

ASMC bulletins are regional situational products. They describe conditions over
a multi-country area and support outlooks over days to weeks.

They do not carry operational obligations for Singapore institutions. A
Singapore preschool's obligations are conditioned on the Singapore 24-hour PSI
band, not on a regional bulletin. ASMC content is useful for anticipating an
episode, and is not a substitute for the local reading when a decision is due.
