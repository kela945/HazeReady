# Tripartite advisory on haze-related workplace measures

MOM, the Singapore National Employers Federation (SNEF) and the National Trades
Union Congress (NTUC) have jointly issued a tripartite advisory to assist
employers in preparing and implementing haze-related workplace measures.

The advisory is tripartite guidance rather than a legal instrument. It sits
alongside the WSH Act duty and does not displace it.

**Coverage note.** The existence and issuing parties of this advisory are
confirmed from MOM's site index. The detailed contents of the advisory document
were not retrieved and are therefore not represented in this corpus. Questions
turning on specific provisions of the tripartite advisory — for example the
treatment of pay or leave for employees stood down during an episode — cannot be
answered from this corpus and should be abstained on.
