# Absence of eldercare-specific published haze thresholds

This is a coverage note authored for this system. It is not an agency
publication and must never be cited as an obligation source.

Cross-agency haze measures published for the 2023 episode name schools (MOE),
preschools (ECDA), workplaces (MOM) and healthcare facilities (MOH). They do not
set out a distinct eldercare or senior-care-centre threshold table equivalent to
the MOM band table for workplaces.

What this means operationally:

- An eldercare facility's decisions are governed by the general
  vulnerable-person health advisory bands, applied to a cohort that is
  vulnerable by definition, plus the healthcare-facility measures on air
  purifiers and patient monitoring.
- Questions demanding an eldercare-specific numeric threshold — "at what PSI
  must a senior care centre suspend its day programme?" — have no published
  answer. The system must abstain and route to the facility's licensing agency,
  rather than reasoning one out by analogy with the school or workplace tables.

Answering such a question by transposing the MOM worksite table onto eldercare
would be confabulation, because the two tables were written for different
populations and different activity profiles.
