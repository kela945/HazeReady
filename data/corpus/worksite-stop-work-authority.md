# Who decides to stop or move outdoor work

No agency issues a stop-work order at a PSI value. The published regime places
the decision with the employer, discharged through risk assessment, and requires
the criteria to have been determined in advance.

Consequences:

- There is no published PSI number that automatically halts outdoor work, and
  supplying one would misstate the regime.
- An organisation that has pre-determined its own criteria is applying its own
  policy, which may be stricter than the published guidance. Its policy governs
  its own operations.
- Employee health feedback is a named input; a worker reporting breathing
  difficulty is a trigger for action regardless of the band.

Questions about pay, leave, or contractual consequences of stood-down work are
employment-terms questions outside the WSH guidance in this corpus.

This is an authored interpretation.
