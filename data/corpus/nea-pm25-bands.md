# 1-hour PM2.5 concentration bands

NEA has published hourly 1-hour PM2.5 concentration readings for the five
regions since 2014, and introduced bandings and descriptors in 2016 to help the
public interpret them.

| Band | Descriptor | 1-hour PM2.5 (µg/m³) | Correlates to 24-hour PSI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Band 1 | Normal | 0 – 55 | 1 – 100 |
| Band 2 | Elevated | 56 – 150 | 101 – 200 |
| Band 3 | High | 151 – 250 | 201 – 300 |
| Band 4 | Very High | above 250 | above 300 |

The band-to-PSI correspondence in the right-hand column is verified against NEA
published material. **The numeric µg/m³ boundaries in the third column are not
verified**: the authoritative table is published on haze.gov.sg, whose
robots.txt disallows automated retrieval by this system, and MOH's site returned
an access-denied response. The boundaries stated here are consistent with an
NEA haze bulletin reporting readings of 30–94 µg/m³ as spanning Band 1 to
Band 2, which places the Band 1/2 boundary between 30 and 94.

Treat the µg/m³ boundaries as indicative. Any operational decision that turns on
a PM2.5 value close to a boundary should be checked against haze.gov.sg
directly. This limitation is recorded in the system's coverage gaps.
