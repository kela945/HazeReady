# Work organisation controls during haze

The controls named in the guidance operate on the work itself, before any
reliance on personal protective equipment:

- **Mechanical aids** in place of manual lifting, reducing exertion and
  therefore respiratory rate and dose.
- **Indoor rest breaks**, so that outdoor exposure is broken up rather than
  continuous.
- **Job rotation**, distributing outdoor exposure across a larger group rather
  than concentrating it.
- **Flexible work arrangements**, including moving work indoors or rescheduling.
- **Hydration.**
- **Monitoring employee health feedback** and acting on it.

The guidance does not specify numeric work-rest cycles for haze — no ratio of
minutes worked to minutes rested is published. Requests for a specific haze
work-rest cycle cannot be answered from this guidance. Singapore's numeric
work-rest framework is published for *heat stress*, which is a separate hazard
with separate triggers, and must not be applied to haze by analogy.
