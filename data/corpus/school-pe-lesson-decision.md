# PE lessons and outdoor training by band

Applying the published school measures to a PE lesson:

| 24-hour PSI | Outdoor PE | Indoor substitute |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 100 | Proceeds normally | Not required |
| 101 – 200 | Minimise — outdoor PE should not proceed as a strenuous session | Permitted, but must **not** be strenuous |
| 201 – 300 | Avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor exertion | Low-intensity indoor activity only |
| above 300 | Minimise outdoor activity | Low-intensity indoor activity only |

The recurring error is treating "move it indoors" as sufficient. The published
instruction at 101–200 pairs *minimise outdoor activities* with *avoid strenuous
indoor physical exercise*. An indoor circuit session at PSI 150 satisfies the
first half and breaches the second.

This is an authored interpretation. The obligations it applies are in
`moe-outdoor-activities-unhealthy` and the NEA health advisory documents; cite
those, not this.
