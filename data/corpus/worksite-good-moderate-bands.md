# Outdoor work at 24-hour PSI 100 and below

At 24-hour PSI 100 and below — the Good and Moderate bands — the guidance calls
for **preparatory measures only**. No restriction on outdoor work applies to
either healthy or susceptible employees at these bands.

This is an explicit permissive statement, and it is the basis on which outdoor
work may proceed normally at these bands. It is stated positively in the
guidance rather than being inferred from the absence of a prohibition.

The preparatory measures that apply here are set out in
`mom-preparatory-measures`: identifying susceptible employees, determining
restriction criteria in advance, mask fit testing and stock, and establishing
employee communications.

Note that the employer's general WSH Act duty is unaffected. "No haze-specific
restriction" is not "no duty".
