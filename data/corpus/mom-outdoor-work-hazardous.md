# Outdoor work measures at 24-hour PSI above 300 (Hazardous)

| Employee group | Required posture |
|---|---|
| Healthy employees | Minimise outdoor work |
| Susceptible employees | Avoid outdoor work |

At the Hazardous band the restriction is no longer qualified by "prolonged or
strenuous". It applies to outdoor work as such. Susceptible employees are to
avoid outdoor work entirely; healthy employees are to minimise it.

Respiratory protection at this band is escalated: employers should provide masks
or appropriate respirators, and the guidance names full face respirators for
prolonged outdoor work at 24-hour PSI above 400.

Where an employee has difficulty breathing while wearing a mask, the employer
should deploy that employee to indoor work rather than continue outdoor
deployment with the mask removed.
