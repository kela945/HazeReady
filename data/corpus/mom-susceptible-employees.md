# Identifying susceptible employees

Employers should identify susceptible employees before an episode. The named
categories are:

- Elderly employees
- Pregnant employees
- Employees with chronic conditions, particularly chronic lung disease and
  heart disease

Identification is a preparatory obligation. It requires a mechanism — usually
voluntary declaration — established in advance, because the classification
determines which of two different postures applies to that individual at
24-hour PSI 101–200.

The guidance does not state an age at which an employee becomes "elderly" for
this purpose, and does not enumerate which conditions count as chronic lung or
heart disease. Where a specific employee's classification is genuinely unclear,
that is a matter for occupational health advice, not for inference from the
published bands.
