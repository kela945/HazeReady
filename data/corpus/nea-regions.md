# Reporting regions for PSI and PM2.5

Readings are reported for five regions plus a national figure:

| Region | Approximate label location (lat, lon) |
|---|---|
| north | 1.41803, 103.82 |
| south | 1.29587, 103.82 |
| east | 1.35735, 103.94 |
| west | 1.35735, 103.70 |
| central | 1.35735, 103.82 |

Regional readings can differ materially during a haze episode because plume
transport is directional. At the time of retrieval the 24-hour PSI was 57
(north), 59 (east), 59 (west), 66 (central), 57 (south) — a spread of 9 index
points across the island in ordinary conditions, and spreads are wider during an
episode.

An institution should apply the reading for the region it is physically in. A
national or island-wide figure understates conditions for the worst-affected
region.
