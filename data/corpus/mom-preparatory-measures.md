# Preparatory measures before a haze episode

At 24-hour PSI 100 and below, the guidance calls for preparatory measures only.
No restriction on outdoor work applies at the Good or Moderate bands.

Preparation consists of:

- Identifying susceptible employees (elderly, pregnant, chronic conditions).
- Determining, in advance, the criteria the organisation will use to restrict
  outdoor work — so that the decision is not improvised under pressure.
- Conducting mask fit testing and maintaining N95 stock.
- Establishing a communication system to reach employees when measures change.

The instruction to set restriction criteria in advance is the substantive one.
An organisation that has not decided beforehand what it will do at each band
will be making safety decisions during an episode with incomplete information
and time pressure.
