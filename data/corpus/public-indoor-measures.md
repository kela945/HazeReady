# Indoor measures during a haze episode

Facilities are advised to deploy air purifiers, fans and portable coolers, and
schools and preschools maintain purifier-equipped rooms.

The underlying principle is that indoor air is not automatically clean air.
Particulate infiltrates. Moving an activity indoors reduces exposure; it does not
eliminate it, which is why the guidance pairs "minimise outdoor activities" with
"avoid strenuous indoor physical exercise" at the Unhealthy band.

The published guidance names the equipment. It does not specify:

- Purifier capacity relative to room volume, or filter class.
- Air-change rates or target indoor PM2.5 levels.
- Whether and when to close windows or run air-conditioning on recirculation.

Specification questions of this kind are engineering questions outside the
published guidance.
