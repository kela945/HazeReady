# Deciding on a next-day school excursion

A decision taken today about tomorrow is a forecast decision, and NEA states the
24-hour PSI forecast is the index suited to forward planning of activities that
run over many hours.

The procedure this implies:

1. Take the **24-hour PSI forecast** for the relevant period, not the current
   reading and not the 1-hour PM2.5.
2. Treat the forecast as a **range**. NEA publishes it as one.
3. Plan against the **worst band the range reaches**, because the obligations
   at that band would apply if it materialises and the excursion cannot be
   unwound mid-way.
4. **Re-check against the actual reading** before departure. A forecast decision
   is provisional.

Where the forecast range straddles 100/101, the governing band for tomorrow is
undetermined, and a decision that must be communicated to parents tonight is
being made under genuine uncertainty. Saying so is more useful than resolving
the range to a point value that the published forecast does not assert.

This is an authored interpretation of the forecast-use guidance.
