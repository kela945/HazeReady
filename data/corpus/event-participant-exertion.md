# Participant exertion at mass-participation outdoor events

The health advisory constrains events through the exertion of participants
rather than through the event as such.

| 24-hour PSI | Constraint on a strenuous mass-participation event |
|---|---|
| 0 – 100 | None from the advisory |
| 101 – 200 | Healthy participants: reduce prolonged or strenuous outdoor exertion. Vulnerable participants: minimise it |
| 201 – 300 | All participants: avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor exertion |
| above 300 | Minimise outdoor activity (healthy); avoid it (vulnerable) |

A competitive road race is prolonged strenuous outdoor exertion for every
participant. At 201–300 the advisory tells every participant to avoid exactly
that, which makes proceeding difficult to reconcile with the advisory even
though no rule prohibits the event.

A static outdoor event with seated attendees is a different exposure profile at
the same reading, and the advisory constrains it less. Event questions cannot be
answered from the PSI band alone without the exertion profile.
