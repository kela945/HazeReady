# Health advisory for healthy persons, by 24-hour PSI band

| 24-hour PSI | Advisory for healthy persons |
|---|---|
| 0 – 100 (Good, Moderate) | Normal activities |
| 101 – 200 (Unhealthy) | Reduce prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion |
| 201 – 300 (Very Unhealthy) | Avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion |
| above 300 (Hazardous) | Minimise outdoor activity |

The wording is deliberately graded. "Reduce" is not "avoid", and "avoid
prolonged or strenuous exertion" is not "avoid going outdoors". Guidance at
101–200 restricts the intensity and duration of outdoor exertion; it does not
prohibit being outdoors.

This advisory applies to the general healthy population. Elderly persons,
pregnant women, children, and persons with chronic lung or heart disease are
covered by a separate and more restrictive advisory.
