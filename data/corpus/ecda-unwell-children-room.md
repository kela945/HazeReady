# Air-conditioned room with air purifier for unwell children

Preschools are required to maintain an air-conditioned room fitted with an air
purifier for children who become unwell during a haze episode.

This is a standing capability requirement rather than a band-triggered action:
the room is to exist and be usable, so that a child who becomes unwell can be
moved into it immediately.

The published requirement specifies the room and the purifier. It does not
specify purifier capacity, room volume, how many children the room must
accommodate, or how long a child may remain in it before the parent must be
called. Those operational details are not in the guidance.
