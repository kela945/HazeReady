# Haze risk assessment requirements for employers

Employers must carry out a proper risk assessment and implement appropriate
measures. The assessment must take three factors together:

1. **The PSI level** — the prevailing and forecast 24-hour PSI band.
2. **Employee health conditions** — whether any employee is elderly, pregnant,
   or has a chronic lung or heart condition.
3. **The intensity of the activity** — light, moderate, or strenuous, and how
   prolonged the outdoor exposure is.

The same PSI band produces different required measures for different work. A
worker doing light supervisory work outdoors for twenty minutes and a worker
doing sustained manual handling outdoors for six hours are not in the same
position at 24-hour PSI 150.

The risk assessment is a documented, prior activity. It is expected to have been
prepared before an episode, not improvised during one.
