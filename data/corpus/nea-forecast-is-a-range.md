# The 24-hour PSI forecast is published as a range, not a point value

NEA haze situation updates report both the observed reading and the forecast as
a *range*, and describe that range in band language rather than as a single
number. A published bulletin reports, for example, that the 24-hour PSI "was
66-120, in the high-end of the Moderate range to low-end of the Unhealthy
range", and forecasts the following 24 hours in the same terms.

Two consequences for anyone planning ahead:

1. A forecast may straddle a band boundary. A forecast of "high-end Moderate to
   low-end Unhealthy" means the governing band for tomorrow is *not yet
   determined*. Planning must be done against the worst band in the range, not
   the midpoint and not the best case.
2. A decision made this evening against a forecast is provisional. It should be
   revisited against the actual reading before the activity begins.

Reporting a forecast as a single number discards the uncertainty that the
published range exists to convey.
