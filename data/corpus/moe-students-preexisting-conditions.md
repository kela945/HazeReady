# Students with pre-existing lung or heart conditions

Teachers monitor for students who are unwell or who have pre-existing lung or
heart conditions. Parents should ensure their children have their medication
with them during a haze situation — for example, inhalers for asthma.

The school's role as published is *monitoring and enabling access to the
student's own medication*. It is not clinical management.

Any question about whether a particular child should take a particular
medication, at what dose, or whether a child's symptoms warrant medical
attention, falls outside this guidance and outside the competence of a school.
Those questions go to the child's healthcare provider. A decision-support system
must route them there rather than answer them.
