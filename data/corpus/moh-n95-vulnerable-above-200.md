# N95 mask advisory for vulnerable persons

Vulnerable persons who need to be outdoors for several hours are advised to wear
an N95 mask when the 24-hour PSI is above 200.

Three qualifiers are load-bearing and are frequently dropped:

1. **Vulnerable persons.** The advisory is directed at elderly persons, pregnant
   women, children, and persons with chronic lung or heart disease.
2. **Several hours outdoors.** It addresses prolonged outdoor exposure, not
   brief trips.
3. **Above 200.** It is not triggered at the Unhealthy band (101–200).

An N95 mask is not a general-population recommendation at every band, and it is
not a substitute for staying indoors where that is possible.
