# School continuity plan during haze

MOE and schools operate a school continuity plan to ensure the well-being of
students and staff during a haze episode. Schools respond and take haze
management measures based on the health advisories issued by MOH corresponding
to the prevailing air quality.

The structure matters for interpreting any school question. MOE does not publish
its own independent PSI thresholds; it publishes a plan that *tracks* the MOH
health advisory. The governing numbers are therefore the health advisory bands,
and the school-specific content is the operational translation of those bands
into activity decisions.

MOE's published page does not contain a numeric PSI-to-action table. Where a
question requires a specific school action at a specific PSI value that is not
covered by the cross-agency measures recorded elsewhere in this corpus, the
guidance is silent and the school should follow MOE's direct instructions to
schools rather than an inference.
