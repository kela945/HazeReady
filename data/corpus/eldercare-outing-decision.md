# Eldercare outings and day-programme activities

Applying the vulnerable-person advisory to an eldercare day centre:

| 24-hour PSI | Outdoor group outing | Indoor programme |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 100 | Proceeds | Normal |
| 101 – 200 | Minimise — a discretionary outing should be postponed | Avoid strenuous indoor exercise |
| 201 – 300 | Avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor exertion; stay indoors where possible | Low intensity |
| above 300 | Avoid outdoor activity; remain indoors | Low intensity |

The centre's own transport arrangements are a separate exposure from the
programme: clients arriving and departing are outdoors briefly at every band.
The advisory addresses this only at Hazardous, where outdoor activity is to be
avoided.

No published guidance sets an eldercare-specific threshold for suspending a day
programme entirely, or for closing a centre. Those questions are not answerable
from this corpus.

This is an authored interpretation applying the general vulnerable-person
advisory; cite the NEA advisory documents rather than this one.
