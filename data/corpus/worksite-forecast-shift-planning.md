# Planning a shift against the 24-hour PSI forecast

Rostering, job rotation and redeployment are decisions taken before the shift,
so they are forecast decisions. The 24-hour PSI forecast is the index NEA
designates for planning activities that run over many hours, which a shift does.

Applying the preparation guidance to a forecast:

- Where the forecast range reaches 101–200, plan rotation and indoor rest
  breaks in advance, and identify which susceptible employees will need
  minimised outdoor work.
- Where it reaches 201–300, plan on the basis that prolonged or strenuous
  outdoor work is to be avoided, and that masks must be available if outdoor
  work proves unavoidable.
- Where it reaches above 300, plan indoor redeployment for susceptible
  employees, since they are to avoid outdoor work entirely at that band.

Plan against the worst band the forecast range reaches. A plan built on the
midpoint leaves no measure in place if the upper end materialises, and the
measures — fit-tested masks, rotation rosters — cannot be assembled at short
notice.
