# Definition of vulnerable groups for haze health advisories

Haze health advisories distinguish two populations:

- **Healthy persons.**
- **Vulnerable persons:** the elderly, pregnant women, children, and persons
  with chronic lung disease or heart disease.

The classification determines which advisory row applies at a given band, and at
24-hour PSI 101–200 the two rows differ ("reduce" versus "minimise").

The published categories are not defined numerically. No age threshold is given
for "elderly" or for "children", no gestational stage is specified, and the set
of qualifying chronic conditions is not enumerated. Where an individual's
classification is genuinely borderline, the guidance does not resolve it and a
healthcare provider should.

For institutional decisions this ambiguity usually does not bite, because the
institution's cohort is unambiguous: a preschool's children and an eldercare
centre's clients are vulnerable on any reading of the categories.
