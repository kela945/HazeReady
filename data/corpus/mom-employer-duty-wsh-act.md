# Employer's primary duty under the WSH Act during haze

Under the Workplace Safety and Health Act, the primary responsibility for
ensuring an employee's safety and health at work lies with the employer. Haze
does not create a separate legal regime; it is a hazard to be managed within the
existing duty.

The duty is discharged through risk assessment and control, not through
compliance with a fixed PSI cut-off. There is no published PSI value at which
outdoor work automatically becomes unlawful. The employer must assess and act.

This matters for how questions about worksites should be answered. "Is it legal
to keep working at PSI 220?" has no yes/no answer in the published guidance.
What the guidance specifies is what the employer must have done: assessed the
risk, identified susceptible employees, and implemented measures proportionate
to the band. An answer that supplies a bare permission or prohibition
misrepresents the regime.
