# Boundary of health advisory: individual medical management is out of scope

Haze health advisories are population-level guidance expressed as activity
recommendations by band. They state what categories of person should do about
outdoor exertion. They do not constitute medical advice for an individual.

Specifically, the published advisories do not address:

- Whether a named individual should start, stop, or change a medication.
- Dosing of relief medication such as an asthma inhaler.
- Whether particular symptoms warrant seeking medical attention, and how
  urgently.
- Fitness of a specific individual to undertake a specific activity.

These are clinical judgements requiring the individual's history, and they
belong with that person's healthcare provider. A decision-support system should
decline them explicitly and route the asker to a clinician, rather than
answering from population guidance.

This document is an explicit scope statement authored for this system. It
records a boundary that the published advisories imply but do not spell out.
