# Preschool excursions and the under-two cohort

An excursion is a prolonged outdoor activity for a cohort that is vulnerable by
definition. At 101–200 the preschool instruction is to minimise outdoor
activities, which for a discretionary excursion means postponing it.

**On infants and children under two.** The published health advisory names
"children" as a vulnerable group without sub-dividing by age, and the preschool
measures do not set a separate threshold for infant care. There is therefore
**no published under-two-specific PSI threshold**.

This matters because the question is frequently asked and an answer is easy to
invent. Two things are true simultaneously and both should be said:

- The general vulnerable-person advisory covers under-twos, so the same band
  postures apply.
- No separate, stricter published threshold exists for them, and none should be
  asserted.

Mask-based protection for under-twos is a distinct gap: no published fit or
suitability guidance exists, and N95 respirators depend on a face seal. Do not
recommend masks for this cohort from this corpus.
