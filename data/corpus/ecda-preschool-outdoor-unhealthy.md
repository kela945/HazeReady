# Preschool outdoor activities at 24-hour PSI 101-200

In the Unhealthy band, preschools are to **minimise outdoor activities** and to
**avoid strenuous indoor physical exercise**, replacing them with alternative
activities.

Preschool cohorts consist entirely of children, who are a vulnerable group under
the NEA health advisory. The vulnerable-person advisory — *minimise* prolonged
or strenuous outdoor physical exertion at 101–200 — is therefore the governing
health advisory for the whole cohort, not just for individual children with
known conditions.

The instruction to *replace* with alternative activities, rather than simply
cancel, reflects the standing requirement that preschools provide daily physical
activity. The requirement does not disappear during haze; it moves indoors and
drops in intensity.
