# Health advisory for vulnerable persons, by 24-hour PSI band

Vulnerable persons are elderly persons, pregnant women, children, and persons
with chronic lung disease or heart disease.

| 24-hour PSI | Advisory for vulnerable persons |
|---|---|
| 0 – 50 (Good) | Normal activities |
| 51 – 100 (Moderate) | Normal activities |
| 101 – 200 (Unhealthy) | Minimise prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion |
| 201 – 300 (Very Unhealthy) | Avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion; stay indoors where possible |
| above 300 (Hazardous) | Avoid outdoor activity; remain indoors |

Vulnerable persons are advised to be more cautious at the same reading than the
healthy population. Where an institution's cohort is wholly or predominantly
vulnerable — a preschool, an eldercare day centre — the vulnerable-person
advisory is the governing one for that institution's activity decisions.

Persons with chronic lung or heart disease should keep their relief medication
available. Guidance on individual medical management is a matter for a
healthcare provider and is not covered by this advisory.
