# Mask use by the general public during haze

The published mask advisory is narrow: N95 masks for vulnerable persons who need
to be outdoors for several hours, when the 24-hour PSI is above 200.

What is *not* published, and must not be inferred:

- A recommendation that the general healthy population wear N95 masks at any
  band.
- Guidance on surgical or cloth masks for haze particulate.
- Fit or sizing guidance for children, including whether N95 masks are suitable
  for young children at all.
- Duration limits for continuous N95 wear.

These gaps are real. An N95 mask relies on a face seal, and seal quality is the
whole mechanism; guidance that does not address fit for a given population is
not guidance that can be extended to that population by assumption.
