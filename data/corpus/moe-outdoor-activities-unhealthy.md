# School outdoor activities at 24-hour PSI 101-200

In the Unhealthy band, schools are to **minimise outdoor activities** and to
**avoid strenuous indoor physical exercise**.

The second half is the part that is routinely missed. Moving a PE lesson indoors
is not by itself compliance. Indoor air is not filtered air, and a strenuous
indoor session raises respiratory rate and therefore dose. Both the outdoor
restriction and the indoor-intensity restriction apply at the same band.

The correct substitution at this band is a *low-intensity* indoor activity, not
an equally strenuous indoor one.

Note the verb: "minimise", not "cancel". Outdoor activity is reduced to what is
necessary rather than prohibited outright at this band.
