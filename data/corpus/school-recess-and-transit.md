# Recess, transit and incidental outdoor exposure

The health advisory restricts *prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical
exertion*. Walking between blocks, queuing at a canteen, or boarding a bus is
neither prolonged nor strenuous, and the advisory does not address it below the
Hazardous band.

At above 300 (Hazardous) the wording changes: healthy persons are to minimise
outdoor activity and vulnerable persons to avoid it. Incidental outdoor movement
is within scope at that band, and a school would be minimising it.

Running games during recess are a different matter. They are strenuous outdoor
exertion, and at 101–200 the school instruction to minimise outdoor activities
applies to them.

The published guidance does not set a duration in minutes at which outdoor
exposure becomes "prolonged". Requests for such a number cannot be satisfied
from the guidance.
