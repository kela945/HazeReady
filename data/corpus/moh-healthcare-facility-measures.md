# Healthcare facility measures during haze

Measures for healthcare and care facilities during an episode:

- Prepare for an increase in haze-related presentations.
- Deploy air purifiers, fans and portable coolers within facilities.
- Monitor patients closely.

These are capability and vigilance measures rather than threshold-triggered
activity restrictions. No PSI value is published at which a care facility must
take a specific operational action such as suspending outings or closing a day
programme.

For an eldercare day centre, this means the governing published numbers are the
general vulnerable-person health advisory bands, applied to a cohort that is
vulnerable by definition. Facility-specific thresholds do not exist in the
published guidance.
