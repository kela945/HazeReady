# Personal outdoor exercise decisions

NEA states explicitly that the 1-hour PM2.5 concentration readings are meant for
immediate decisions such as whether or not to go for a walk or run outdoors.

A run is prolonged strenuous outdoor exertion. Applying the advisory:

- **Band 1 / Normal:** no advisory constraint.
- **Band 2 / Elevated** (corresponds to PSI 101–200): healthy runners reduce
  duration and intensity; vulnerable runners minimise it.
- **Band 3 / High** (corresponds to PSI 201–300): avoid.
- **Band 4 / Very High:** minimise outdoor activity generally.

A walk and a run are different exposures at the same reading, because
ventilation rate scales with intensity. The advisory language reflects this by
restricting *strenuous* exertion rather than being outdoors.
