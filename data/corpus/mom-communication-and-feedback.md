# Communicating haze measures to employees

Employers should implement a communication system with employees, established
before an episode, covering:

- What the current band is and where the reading comes from.
- Which measures are now in force and which employees they apply to.
- How an employee reports difficulty breathing or other symptoms, and what
  happens when they do.
- Who has authority to stop or move work.

Employee health feedback is named in the guidance as an input to be monitored,
which implies a route by which it reaches a decision-maker. A communication
system that only broadcasts downward does not satisfy this.
