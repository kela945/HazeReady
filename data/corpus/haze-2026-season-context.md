# 2026 transboundary haze season context

Singapore lies downwind of Sumatra and Kalimantan during the southwest monsoon,
and the June–September window is when transboundary smoke from land and forest
fires historically reaches the island.

**This document is context only and is deliberately thin.** The 2026 seasonal
risk rating and outlook are not reproduced here, because no agency source for
them was retrieved and verified by this system. Statements about the severity of
the current season are therefore not in this corpus and cannot be cited from it.

Nothing in the seasonal outlook changes an operational obligation. Obligations
are conditioned on the prevailing 24-hour PSI band and the health advisory, not
on a seasonal forecast. A question of the form "given this year's outlook,
should we cancel the term's outdoor programme?" is a planning question that the
published guidance does not answer.
