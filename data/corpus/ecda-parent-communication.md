# Communicating haze measures to parents

Preschools are expected to keep parents informed of the measures in force during
a haze episode, including changes to outdoor programming and arrangements for
children who become unwell.

**Coverage note.** This document records an operational expectation that follows
from the published requirements to monitor unwell children and to ensure
children have their medication available. It is *not* a verbatim published ECDA
communications protocol, and no specific notification timing, channel, or
template is published.

Questions about *when* parents must be notified, in what form, or whether a
centre may change programming without prior notice, are not answerable from
published guidance. Treat this document as context, not as an obligation source,
and do not cite it as the basis for a specific communication requirement.
