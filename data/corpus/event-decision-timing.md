# When an event cancellation decision has to be made

An event decision is made before the event, against a forecast, and usually
before the forecast has resolved. The organiser's risk assessment is the
mechanism, and the 24-hour PSI forecast is the stated input.

Practical structure:

1. **Ahead of the event:** assess against the forecast range, planning for the
   worst band it reaches.
2. **Publish the criteria:** participants should know in advance what reading
   triggers what change, so a decision on the day is not read as arbitrary.
3. **On the day:** re-check the actual reading. For an event running several
   hours, the 24-hour PSI remains the relevant index; the 1-hour PM2.5 informs
   whether conditions are deteriorating within the event window.

No published guidance sets a cancellation threshold, a notice period, or refund
or make-up obligations. Those are commercial and contractual matters outside
this corpus.
