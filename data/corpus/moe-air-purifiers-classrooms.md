# Air purifier provision in schools

All classrooms are equipped with air purifiers as part of the standing haze
preparedness posture.

This is an infrastructure statement, not a threshold-triggered action. The
purifiers are present; the published guidance does not specify a PSI value at
which they are switched on, nor a room size, air-change rate, filter class, or
maintenance interval.

Questions about purifier *sizing*, *selection*, or *filter specification* are
not answerable from this corpus. They are engineering questions that the
published guidance does not address, and answering them would require inventing
a specification. This is recorded as a coverage gap.
