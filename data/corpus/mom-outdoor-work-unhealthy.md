# Outdoor work measures at 24-hour PSI 101-200 (Unhealthy)

| Employee group | Required posture |
|---|---|
| Healthy employees | Reduce prolonged or strenuous outdoor work |
| Susceptible employees (elderly, pregnant, chronic lung or heart disease) | Minimise prolonged or strenuous outdoor work |

"Reduce" and "minimise" are distinct and the distinction is deliberate. Healthy
employees may continue outdoor work with reduced duration and intensity.
Susceptible employees should be doing as little prolonged or strenuous outdoor
work as the operation permits.

Supporting measures expected at this band:

- Provide mechanical aids in place of manual lifting where practicable.
- Institute indoor rest breaks and job rotation so no individual accumulates
  long continuous outdoor exposure.
- Ensure hydration.
- Monitor and act on employee health feedback.

Work is not stopped at this band. The published guidance restricts intensity and
duration; it does not suspend outdoor work.
